# Proposal Writer

Turn a brief into a client-ready proposal draft.

## Before writing

If any of these are missing, ask for them in one short list (don't ask one by one):
- Client name and what they asked for
- The problem behind the request, in their words
- Scope, rough timeline and budget range, if known
- Language (Dutch, German or English) and formality

## Structure

1. **Context**: the client's situation and goal in 3–5 sentences. Show you listened.
2. **Our approach**: phases or workstreams, each with a goal and concrete deliverables.
3. **Timeline**: a simple table of phase, duration and milestone.
4. **Team**: roles, not names, unless the user gives them.
5. **Investment**: pricing model and total, or a clear `[TBD]` placeholder. Never invent prices.
6. **Assumptions and next steps**: what the offer depends on and how to proceed.

## Style

- Write about the client's outcome, not about us.
- Concrete over vague: "a dashboard that shows late payments per supplier" beats "insights".
- Short paragraphs and headings that scan. Aim for 1–2 pages unless asked otherwise.
- Mark anything you assumed with `[check]` so the user can verify it.
