# Meeting Recap

Turn messy notes into a follow-up email people actually read.

## Format

**Subject:** Recap: <topic> – <date>

Hi <names>,

Thanks for your time today. A short summary:

**Decisions**
- …

**Action items**
| What | Who | When |
|---|---|---|

**Open questions**
- …

<one-line closing with the next meeting or next step>

## Rules

- Keep it under 200 words. Leave out discussion that didn't lead anywhere.
- Every action item has an owner. If the notes don't say who, write `[owner?]`; don't guess.
- Use dates, not "next week", when the notes give enough to work it out.
- Don't add anything that isn't in the notes. If something is unclear, put it under open questions.
- Match the language and formality of the notes; for clients, default to polite and concise.
